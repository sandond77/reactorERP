Sales

Every completed sale. One row per sales entry. Filter by platform, date range, card type.

What you see

Columns: Date sold, Cert/ID, Card, Grade/Condition, Qty, Sale method (platform), Order link, Raw cost, Grading cost, Listed price, Strike price, After fees, Net (profit), CS Price (if card show).

Common actions

  • + Record Sale — log a completed sale. Pick the card (search by cert # or card name), pick the platform, fill in sale price + fees + shipping. For eBay: paste the order URL — the order # auto-fills from the URL's orderid param. For card show: pick the show + day.
  • Bulk Record Sale — for card shows. Add multiple cards in one cart, hit the total, splits across cards proportionally.
  • Click a row — opens the sale detail modal. Edit any field; cost basis recomputes if you change the price/fees.
  • Delete sale — restores the underlying card to inventory (graded → graded; raw → raw quantity refund). The confirmation label tells you which.

Filters worth knowing

  • All / Graded / Raw top tabs.
  • Platform filter — eBay, TCGPlayer, Card Show, Facebook, Instagram, Local, Other.
  • Card show filter — narrow to sales from a specific show.
  • Date range — sold date.

Editable inline

The CS Price (card show sticker price) and Listed Price are editable inline from the sale detail modal — they're per-card properties, not sale-row properties, but it's the only place to set them without navigating away.
