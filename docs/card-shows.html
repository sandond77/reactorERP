Card Show Inventory

How to prep for a card show, track on-show inventory separately from your main listings, and report on the day(s).

1. Set up the show

Card Shows → New Show. Name it, set the date(s) (single or multi-day), location.

2. Add inventory

Card Show Inventory → Add to Card Show.

  • Graded slabs: search by cert # or card name. Pick the cards you're bringing. Each gets is_card_show = true and an optional card_show_price (the sticker price you're hoping to get).
  • Raw: same flow, slightly different table (raw cards have condition, no cert).

Cards on the show table aren't hidden from your main inventory — they're still listed as available. They carry the on-show flag so:

  • The eBay listing flow shows an "On Show" badge so you don't double-list one.
  • The Card Show Inventory page can show only on-show cards.
  • Reports can filter by show.

3. During the show

Record sales as they happen via Sales → Record Sale → Card Show platform. Pick the show + day. The sale uses the card show price as the default.

You can also bulk-record sales after the fact from the Card Show Inventory page.

4. After the show

Reports → Card Show Breakdown → [your show]. You get:

  • Slab + raw counts
  • Gross revenue
  • Net (after fees, though card show fees are usually 0 unless you split)
  • Total cost basis
  • Profit

For multi-day shows, you can pick a specific day or see the overall.

The AI Agent can also generate this report on demand: ask it "what's my P&L for the [show name]?"

Removing cards from the show

After the show:

  • Card Show Inventory → Raw or Graded tab → click a row → Remove from Card Show. The card flips is_card_show back to false and clears the sticker price.
  • Or bulk-remove via the Remove All Unsold button.

Unsold cards return to your main inventory automatically — they were never removed, just flagged.
