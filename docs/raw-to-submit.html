To Be Submitted

Holding bay for raw cards routed to grading but not yet packed into a grading batch. Inspect, decide what's worth the fee, build the batch.

What you see

Every raw card_instance with decision = 'grade' and status inspected or raw_for_sale that hasn't been added to a grading_submission yet.

Columns: Card name, Set, Card #, Condition, Notes, Cost, Source lot, Date inspected.

Common actions

  • Filter / search — narrow by card name, set, condition, source lot.
  • Move to a new submission — select rows, click Add to Batch. Pick an existing submission or create a new one. Cards flip to grading_submitted status.
  • Change decision — click a row → set decision to sell_raw if you change your mind. Card moves to Raw Overall as for-sale inventory.

Tips

  • This view is useful when you accumulate slabs-worth of cards over time and want to batch them efficiently for one shipment.
  • For pricing strategy: filter by condition and look at the raw market price vs expected slab price + grading fee. Cards that don't clear the spread shouldn't be submitted.
