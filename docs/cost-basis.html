Cost Basis

Cost basis is the source of truth for "what did I actually pay for this card." Reactor tracks it through every state transition so profit numbers stay accurate even after grading, splitting, or partial sales.

Raw

A raw purchase has a total_cost_usd for the whole order. When the lot is split during inspection into sub-rows by condition, the cost is allocated proportionally by quantity:

Bulk lot: 50 cards, $1,000 total → $20/card
Split into:
  24 NM @ $480
  20 LP @ $400
  6  MP @ $120

Each split gets its own purchase_cost (per-card) and contributes proportionally.

Graded

When a raw card is submitted to grading and comes back as a slab:

slab.raw_cost     = raw card's purchase_cost (allocated from lot)
slab.grading_cost = grading fee + any per-card sub fees
slab.total_cost   = raw_cost + grading_cost

For pre-graded slabs purchased directly (no raw history), raw_cost = full purchase price and grading_cost = 0.

Sale

When a sale is recorded:

sales.total_cost_basis = the card's total cost at the moment of sale
sales.net_proceeds     = sale_price − platform_fees − shipping_cost
profit                 = net_proceeds − total_cost_basis

total_cost_basis is snapshotted onto the sale row at sale time. Changing the card's cost later (e.g. editing a grading submission's fee) also recomputes historical sales' total_cost_basis if the slab is sold — so profit reports stay accurate.

What "must reconcile" means

If you take a raw lot's total cost and follow every card forward — raw sales, grading transformations, slab sales, write-offs — the dollars should add up. No orphaned cost, no lost cost, no double-counting.

The places this matters most:

  • Grading submissions: edits to grading_cost fan out to every linked slab AND every related historical sale.
  • Inspection splits: per-card cost = lot cost / lot quantity. Splitting doesn't change the lot's total.
  • Returns / reverts: if you delete a grading batch, slabs are removed and source raw cards restored from the audit log.

What it doesn't mean

  • Card show fees, postage, etc. don't reduce cost basis. They live on expenses and hit overall P&L, not per-card profit.
  • Listed price != sale price. Listed price is aspirational. total_cost_basis only matters at sale time.
  • CS Price (card show sticker) is a display/intent price for the show, not cost basis.
