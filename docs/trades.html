Trades

Card-for-card swaps. Track what went out, what came in, and net cash if there was boot involved.

What you see

One row per trade. Columns: Date, Counterparty, Out value, In value, Net cash (positive = you got cash, negative = you paid), Notes.

Each row expands to show the cards on each side.

Common actions

  • + New Trade — start a trade. Pick "Out" cards from your inventory + "In" cards (which create new card_instances at the agreed cost). Set net cash if applicable.
  • Click a row — opens the trade detail. Edit either side, change cash, delete the trade.

Cost basis on traded cards

  • Out cards: removed from inventory. The trade records their cost basis at the trade moment so historical P&L stays right.
  • In cards: created at the agreed value as their cost basis. The "value" you assign becomes their purchase_cost.
  • Net cash: a separate accounting line; doesn't affect per-card cost.

Tips

  • If you're trading down (smaller value out, bigger value in) and paying cash, set net cash as a negative number. P&L treats it like a partial purchase.
  • Trades are a low-friction way to clean out raw inventory you don't want to ship to grading.
