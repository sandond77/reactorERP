Card Show Inventory

Cards currently flagged on a card show table. Two tabs: Graded and Raw.

What you see

Inventory rows where is_card_show = true. Same columns as Graded/Slabs or Raw Overall, plus a CS Price column (per-card sticker price).

Common actions

  • + Add to Card Show — opens a search modal. Search by cert # or card name, pick cards, set their CS prices. Max 5 cards per add operation. The flag flips is_card_show = true and stamps card_show_added_at.
  • Click a row — opens the slab/raw detail modal in card-show mode. The only editable field is CS Price. The Delete button becomes Remove from Card Show (flips the flag back, clears the price).

How on-show inventory differs

  • Cards stay in your main inventory — the show flag doesn't hide them from Graded/Slabs or Raw Overall.
  • The eBay listing flow shows an On Show badge on certs flagged on-show. FIFO auto-pick prefers off-show certs to avoid double-listing.
  • The Sales record-flow can pre-fill the CS Price when platform = Card Show.

After the show

Use the per-row Remove from Card Show or bulk remove unsold cards from the page. Unsold cards return to your main listings unchanged; sold cards already moved to sold status when you recorded the sale.
