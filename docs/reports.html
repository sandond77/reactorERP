Reports

Aggregate views of the business — sales by period, by platform, by show, by grade. Drill-downs.

Available reports

Sales by Month

Revenue, fees, cost basis, profit, ROI per month. Filterable by year, platform, card type.

Sales by Platform

Same metrics rolled up by platform (eBay, TCGPlayer, Card Show, etc). Useful for seeing fee burden by channel.

Card Show Breakdown

Per-show summary: slab count + raw count + total inventory value brought, gross revenue, net revenue, total cost basis, profit. Multi-day shows let you slice by day.

The AI Agent can generate this on demand: "what's my P&L for the [show name]?"

Grading ROI

For every grading submission, the ROI of cards that came back graded and were subsequently sold. Useful for evaluating whether a service level is worth the price.

Inventory Aging

How long each unsold card has been in inventory. Group by buckets (0–30, 30–90, 90–180, 180+ days). Surfaces dead stock.

Common actions

  • Filter — date range, platform, card type (graded/raw), specific show.
  • Export CSV — most reports have a download button.
  • Click a row — drills into the underlying sales/inventory list.

Tips

  • Reports use snapshotted total_cost_basis from the sale row. Editing a card's cost basis after sale recomputes the sale's basis and the report updates accordingly.
  • For tax season: Sales by Month + Expenses gives you the two halves of a P&L statement.
