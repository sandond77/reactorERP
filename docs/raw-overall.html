Raw Overall

A roll-up of every raw lot showing its commerce breakdown — what was split into Sell Raw, what was submitted to grading, what's sold, what's unsold.

What you see

One row per raw lot (raw_purchases entry). Columns: Part #, ID (purchase_id), Card name, Total qty, Unsold qty, Sold qty, Condition, Location, Listed?, Listed price, Cost, Strike price, Net, ROI, dates.

Click the chevron on a lot to expand its sub-row breakdown:

  • SOLD — sub-rows showing each completed sale from this lot.
  • FOR SALE — current unsold raw inventory split by condition.
  • GRADING SUBMITTED — what's in flight to a grader.
  • LOST/DAMAGED — write-offs from this lot.

Sub-rows for graded, inspected, and purchased_raw statuses are hidden — graded children are in Graded / Slabs; inspected/purchased are intermediate states that aren't useful here.

Tabs at the top

  • Summary — high-level totals.
  • All / Unsold / Sold / For Sale / To Grade / Submitted — filter the lot list by aggregate status.

Tips

  • Cost reconciliation — Total qty × per-card cost should equal the lot's total_cost_usd. If it doesn't, the lot was edited after splitting.
  • Click a sub-row to open the card_instance detail modal.
  • Card Show indicator — sub-rows where is_card_show = true show a "Card Show" label.
