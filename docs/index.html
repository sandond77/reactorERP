Reactor

Trading card inventory ERP, Pokemon focus. Manages the full lifecycle from purchase to sale across raw cards and graded slabs.

If you sell trading cards and you've been tracking inventory in spreadsheets, Reactor is a more honest answer to the lifecycle questions you keep running into:

  • What did I pay for this specific cert, including grading fees allocated from the original raw lot?
  • How much raw inventory is sitting in this bulk lot vs. how much has been split out?
  • This sale was a set listing — did all components actually ship?
  • What's my P&L for this card show — slabs + raw, fees included?

Reactor models inventory state transitions, lot traceability, and cost basis as first-class concerns. Spreadsheets pretend they do; usually they don't.
