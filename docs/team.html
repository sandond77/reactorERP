Team

Organization membership. Add collaborators to your Reactor org so they can see and update your inventory.

How orgs work

Every user is the owner of their own organization by default. Inventory is scoped to an organization_id. When you invite someone to your org, they share access to your inventory — they sign in with their own account, see your data.

Under the hood: req.dataUserId resolves to the org owner's user_id for org members. So org members read/write the same data as the owner.

Common actions

  • Invite member — send an invite via email. They sign in with Google OAuth; if their account exists, they join immediately. Otherwise the invite waits until they sign up.
  • Remove member — they lose access to your org's inventory. Their own org (if they have one) is untouched.
  • Transfer ownership — pass org ownership to another member. Rare; useful when handing off the business.

Roles (current state)

Currently there's only one role — full read/write. Future versions may add role granularity (e.g. read-only, no-delete).

Tips

  • Multiple people working the same inventory? One person owns the org; the rest are members.
  • If you want to run multiple businesses with separate inventories, sign up multiple accounts — each gets its own org. Org boundaries are hard walls; data never crosses.
